<h1 align="center">🧠 Autonomous Adaptive Portfolio & Risk Management Engine</h1>

<p align="center">
<b>Institutional-Grade Adaptive Portfolio Intelligence</b><br>
Regime-Aware • Risk-First • Leakage-Free • Stress-Tested
</p>

<hr>

<h2>🧩 Problem Statement</h2>

<p>
This project builds an <b>Autonomous Adaptive Portfolio & Risk Management Engine</b> —
not a stock prediction model or buy/sell signal generator.
</p>

<p>
Traditional portfolio strategies assume markets behave consistently over time.
However, financial markets continuously shift between:
</p>

<ul>
<li>📈 Trending Markets</li>
<li>↔️ Sideways Markets</li>
<li>🌪 High-Volatility Periods</li>
<li>💥 Crisis / Crash Conditions</li>
</ul>

<p>
A <b>static allocation strategy fails across regimes</b>.
</p>

<p>
The real challenge is not maximizing returns — 
it is <b>dynamically managing risk under changing market conditions</b>.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>🎯 System Requirements</h2>

<ul>
<li>✅ Detect market regime shifts</li>
<li>✅ Adapt asset allocation dynamically</li>
<li>✅ Control downside risk proactively</li>
<li>✅ Avoid data leakage in backtesting</li>
<li>✅ Remain stable during stress events</li>
<li>✅ Provide transparent reasoning for decisions</li>
</ul>

<p>
This transforms the problem from a prediction task into a 
<b>complete institutional-grade portfolio management framework</b>.
</p>

<hr>

<hr>

<h2>🏗 System Components</h2>

<h3>1️⃣ Data Engineering (Leakage-Free)</h3>

<p><b>Objective:</b> Ensure all decisions use strictly past information.</p>

<ul>
<li>Historical price & return collection</li>
<li>Rolling volatility, momentum, drawdown, correlations</li>
<li>Proper shifting to eliminate lookahead bias</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Principle:</b> No future data is ever used.</p>

<hr>

<h3>2️⃣ Regime Detection Engine</h3>

<p>Markets are dynamically classified into:</p>

<ul>
<li>🟢 Normal</li>
<li>📈 Trending Up</li>
<li>📉 Trending Down</li>
<li>🌪 High Volatility</li>
<li>🔴 Crash</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Indicators Used:</b></p>

<ul>
<li>Rolling volatility</li>
<li>Drawdown levels</li>
<li>Trend strength</li>
<li>Momentum signals</li>
</ul>

<p>
Regime classification directly influences portfolio behavior.
</p>

<hr>

<h3>3️⃣ Dynamic Allocation Engine</h3>

<p>Allocation adjusts based on detected regime.</p>

<ul>
<li>Adaptive asset weight adjustments</li>
<li>Risk parity / mean-variance optimization</li>
<li>Defensive rotation during high-risk periods</li>
<li>Risk scaling during stable regimes</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Allocation is adaptive — not fixed.</b></p>

<hr>

<h3>4️⃣ Risk Management Layer (Core Component)</h3>

<p>Risk controls override return optimization when necessary.</p>

<ul>
<li>🎯 Volatility targeting</li>
<li>📉 Drawdown-based exposure reduction</li>
<li>📏 Position sizing constraints</li>
<li>🛑 Stop-loss logic</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Primary Objective:</b> Capital preservation during adverse conditions.</p>

<hr>

<h3>5️⃣ Backtesting & Validation Framework</h3>

<ul>
<li>Walk-forward validation</li>
<li>Chronological train-test splits</li>
<li>Transaction cost modeling</li>
<li>Strict no-lookahead enforcement</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Key Metrics:</b></p>

<ul>
<li>📊 Sharpe Ratio</li>
<li>📊 Sortino Ratio</li>
<li>📉 Maximum Drawdown</li>
<li>📈 CAGR</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h3>6️⃣ Stress Testing Module</h3>

<p>Simulated adverse scenarios include:</p>

<ul>
<li>💥 Sudden market shocks</li>
<li>🌪 Volatility spikes</li>
<li>🔗 Correlation breakdowns</li>
</ul>

<p>
Purpose: <b>Validate crash resilience and capital protection capability.</b>
</p>

<hr>

<h2>🛡 Final Objective</h2>

<ul>
<li>Adapt intelligently to market changes</li>
<li>Prioritize risk management over raw returns</li>
<li>Maintain stable performance across regimes</li>
<li>Survive extreme stress conditions</li>
<li>Produce transparent, explainable decisions</li>
</ul>
